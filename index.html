Family Nest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Family Nest ("the App") is a pregnancy, birth, feeding and baby-growth tracker developed and published by Novaware Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what data the App handles, where it goes, and your rights.

In short: everything you log stays on your phone. We have no accounts and no servers that receive your family's data. The only things that leave your phone are the ones you choose to send (to your partner's phone, a backup file, a PDF) and an anonymous purchase ID used for Pro.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not run accounts or servers that store your data.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone or location.
  • We do not sell, share or rent any data.

3. Data that leaves your phone, only when you choose

Partner sync

Partner sync copies your logs between your phone and your partner's phone directly, over your local Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth. There is no server in between. After pairing with a 6-digit code, the data is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key only the two phones know. Alarms and settings are never synced.

You can also send a file through your phone's share sheet (for example Quick Share, AirDrop, a messaging app or email). Once you are paired, that file is encrypted for your partner. Before pairing it is not encrypted, and the App warns you before sending it. Files sent this way travel through the service you pick, under that service's own privacy policy.

Backups and PDF reports

"Back up data" saves a file to a place you choose (for example Downloads or Google Drive). The PDF report for your pediatrician is created on your phone and shared only where you send it.

Android backup

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include the App's data in your device backup (stored in your own Google account, encrypted by Google when your phone has a screen lock) and in phone-to-phone transfers. The partner-sync identity and keys are excluded. You can turn Android backup off in your phone's settings.

4. Third-party services

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage purchases (Family Nest Pro and tips). RevenueCat processes an anonymous, randomly generated user ID and purchase records from Google Play. It receives no family data and nothing that identifies you personally from us.

Update check (GitHub Pages)

The App downloads a small file from this website (hosted on GitHub Pages) to learn whether an update is recommended or required. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to GitHub. No other data is sent. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Google Play

The App is distributed through Google Play, which may collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Storage and security

Your data is stored in the App's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Partner sync traffic is encrypted after pairing. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the App, so we recommend a screen lock.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete everything: uninstall the App, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Family Nest → Storage → Clear data.
  • Delete records: delete any feed, weight, visit or labor entry in the App. With partner sync, the deletion reaches your partner's phone at the next sync.
  • Partner sync: your partner's phone keeps its own copy. Unpairing stops future syncs; your partner can delete their copy on their phone.
  • Backup files and PDFs you saved or sent are under your control; delete them where you stored them.
  • RevenueCat: purchase records are kept for transaction integrity. Contact us to request deletion of your anonymous ID.

8. Children

Family Nest is meant for parents and parents-to-be. It is not directed at children. Information about a baby is entered by their parents and stays on the parents' phones.

9. Not medical advice

Percentiles, projections and other figures in the App are estimates to help you keep track. They are not a diagnosis. Always follow your doctor, midwife or pediatrician.

10. Your rights (GDPR / EEA)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability (the backup file exports all your data), and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because your data stays on your devices, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. For anything else, write to hello@novawarestudio.com.
